What's a Passkey?

A passkey lets you sign in using your fingerprint, face, or device PIN instead of a password. It's more secure and easier to use.

Steps:

  1. Click the button below to register your passkey
  2. Sign in with your temporary password (check your email)
  3. Follow the prompts to create your passkey
  4. Next time, sign in with just your fingerprint or face!
